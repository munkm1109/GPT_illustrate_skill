NAME: Joseon men's hanbok
ALIASES: men's hanbok; Joseon male hanbok; jeogori and baji; durumagi; 남자 한복; 조선 남자 한복
CATEGORY: fashion / traditional clothing

FUNCTION:
- establishes Joseon identity through layered robe, jacket, and trouser logic
- lets the rider read as a disguised or cross-dressed noble youth while staying fully clothed and dignified

CORE_SILHOUETTE:
- short upper jacket under a longer outer robe with broad sleeves and a wrapped front opening over loose trousers
- seated silhouette should read as layered cloth planes draping over the saddle, not a single dress mass

PRIMARY_FORMS:
- jeogori upper jacket
- baji trousers gathered toward the lower leg
- longer outer robe or po / durumagi layer
- collar and tie details
- sleeve volume and hem drape

DIMENSIONAL_ENVELOPE:
- upper body layer stays close enough to show torso direction, while outer robe gives wider side silhouette
- trouser volume gathers at the seat and knees when mounted

THICKNESS_NOTES:
- robe edges remain fabric-thin with visible fold breaks
- sleeves have soft volume but should not become balloon blobs
- seated cloth stacks over the saddle in layered wedges

PROPORTION_RULES:
- jeogori is shorter than the outer robe
- front closure and collar line should create a clear V or crossing wrap logic
- trousers remain roomy but taper toward lower leg or tucked area for riding clarity

HUMAN_SCALE_READ:
- immediately reads as traditional Korean menswear when collar, tie, sleeve, and trouser volume are preserved together
- on a youthful rider, the clothes should remain slightly oversized and elegant rather than sharply tailored modern fashion

SUPPORT_LOGIC:
- cloth must respond to seated side-saddle posture: weight compresses under the seated hip, near-side hem droops, far-side folds bridge over the saddle
- outer robe should not hide the fact that the pelvis is supported by the saddle tree

MATERIALS:
- lightly structured woven cloth such as ramie, cotton, or silk-like fantasy fabric
- matte body with limited highlights

VISUAL_CUES:
- white collar strip and wrap front
- broad but not shapeless sleeves
- layered robe over roomy trousers

ANGLE_NOTES:
- side/3/4 view is best for reading layered drape over the saddle
- front view can flatten the seated layers unless fold groups are clearly separated

DO_NOT_BREAK:
- do not collapse the hanbok into a generic kimono robe
- do not erase the trouser component entirely
- do not render all folds as soft tubes; keep directional breaks and layered planes

STYLE_VARIATION:
- realistic: visible collar, tie, seam direction, layered drape, moderate fabric weight
- anime simplified: cleaner fold families and larger silhouette planes while preserving layered identity
- reference-derived wrapper: keep robe planes graphic and directional so they support focal flow instead of becoming fluffy costume mass

SCENE_USAGE:
- reinforces period identity without needing heavy armor or extra props
- lets pale hair, blue eyes, and black gat contrast against more muted cloth masses
- seated drape can help connect the rider visually to the saddle and tiger shoulder mass
