NAME: Joseon gat
ALIASES: gat; heukrip; black gat; 조선 갓; 흑립
CATEGORY: fashion / headwear

FUNCTION:
- signals Joseon male dress code, refinement, and social presence
- gives the rider a strong circular silhouette and shadow rim around the face

CORE_SILHOUETTE:
- cylindrical crown above a broad gently down-curved brim
- reads as a light but expansive circular hat with translucent edge logic

PRIMARY_FORMS:
- crown
- wide brim
- chin ties or hanging strings

DIMENSIONAL_ENVELOPE:
- brim width clearly exceeds head width and often projects past the shoulders
- crown is narrower than the brim and slightly tapered or cylindrical

THICKNESS_NOTES:
- brim should feel thin and elegant, not like a thick felt disk
- crown wall is light and delicate

PROPORTION_RULES:
- brim dominates the silhouette
- crown sits centered and smaller above the brim disk
- avoid tiny-brim modern hat proportions

HUMAN_SCALE_READ:
- immediately reads as a formal Joseon male hat
- in anime stylization, the brim must still feel broad enough to cast a visible facial shadow band

SUPPORT_LOGIC:
- hat sits above tied hair and is stabilized by strings under or below the jaw
- when the head turns, brim ellipse must tilt consistently with skull orientation

MATERIALS:
- fine bamboo / horsehair woven body with black lacquered read
- translucent dark sheen rather than opaque felt

VISUAL_CUES:
- wide black brim
- high but slender crown
- elegant translucency
- delicate hanging ties

ANGLE_NOTES:
- front: brim width and crown height define identity
- side: brim curve and crown-cylinder relation matter most
- 3/4: best for showing face shadow and silhouette authority

DO_NOT_BREAK:
- do not shrink the brim into a generic round hat
- do not make the material thick or floppy
- do not turn it into a conical bamboo hat

STYLE_VARIATION:
- realistic: translucent lacquer, subtle woven texture, refined tie details
- anime simplified: clean crown/brim read with only slight translucency and selective specular edge
- reference-derived wrapper: brim shadow and black circular silhouette should reinforce facial pressure and composition

SCENE_USAGE:
- excellent for framing large eyes beneath a dark arc
- strengthens Joseon identity even in a fantasy-anime treatment
- pairs well with pale hair because the brim creates a strong dark/light contrast

SOURCE_NOTES:
- Credible reference: encyclopedia describes the gat as a Joseon adult male hat consisting of crown and broad brim, often black-lacquered, with ties and wide stylistic variation through the era
- Inference: for drawability, keep a broad brim, slim crown, and light translucent material logic rather than museum-level construction detail
