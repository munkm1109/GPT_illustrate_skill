NAME: warning panel
ALIASES: warning sign panel; hazard panel; caution panel; alert display; 경고 패널; 위험 패널
CATEGORY: industrial / control signage

FUNCTION:
- communicates danger, restriction, equipment state, or access level
- implies rules, system control, procedural tension, and institutional authority

CORE_SILHOUETTE:
- flat rectangle or vertical plate
- often mounted to a wall, machine face, frame, or hanging support
- readable by a strong border, icon block, and label zones

PRIMARY_FORMS:
- main panel surface
- border or mounting frame
- icon field
- text field
- status light or small screen
- wall or machine mount

DIMENSIONAL_ENVELOPE:
- small caution label logic: roughly 57 x 114 mm to 89 x 127 mm
- common warning sign logic: roughly 178 x 254 mm
- machine-adjacent warning/control panel logic: roughly 150-400 mm wide depending on whether it is pure signage or a hybrid display panel

THICKNESS_NOTES:
- adhesive or printed sign layer reads very thin
- mounted plate or framed warning panel can read 1-5 mm plate thickness plus bracket thickness
- illuminated panel edge or bezel should stay secondary to the face unless intentionally emphasized

PROPORTION_RULES:
- warning hierarchy depends on a strong header band plus message field
- width generally exceeds height for small labels, while tall control panels may flip that ratio
- the border/frame should organize the sign, not dominate it

HUMAN_SCALE_READ:
- small labels read at hand scale near handles, ports, and access covers
- medium signs read at head or torso notice scale on doors and machines
- larger framed warning panels read as machine-face components rather than sticker labels

SUPPORT_LOGIC:
- panels must attach to a real surface, frame, or hinge
- if floating off the wall, they need a visible mount, arm, or bracket
- illuminated panels need a power source implication
- large panels should align with the machine or wall logic around them

MATERIALS:
- coated metal plate
- acrylic cover or display glass
- LED indicator light
- printed vinyl, paint, or etched graphics

VISUAL_CUES:
- hazard stripes
- warning icons
- red or amber indicator light
- serial text blocks
- access numbers
- scratched edges or grime for wear
- header band that clearly signals danger / warning / caution class

ANGLE_NOTES:
- front: best for icon clarity and readable panel hierarchy
- side: panel thickness and mounting method become important
- 3/4: best for integrating the panel into scene depth and showing both glow and mounting

DO_NOT_BREAK:
- panels need believable attachment
- icon/text zones should feel organized, not randomly scattered
- glow should support readability, not erase panel structure
- scale should match nearby machinery or doors
- color hierarchy should still read as a warning system, not generic decoration

STYLE_VARIATION:
- realistic: denser regulatory labels, wear, screws, barcode or serial information
- anime simplified: reduce text amount but keep icon block + status light + border hierarchy
- gothic: carved frame, emblem-like iconography, warning as ritual or sigil language
- neon sci-fi: sharper UI blocks, LED strips, access colors, translucent overlays

SCENE_USAGE:
- adds worldbuilding without needing large volume
- useful near doors, tanks, consoles, and containment systems
- can echo accent colors from the face or key light without stealing primary focus
- helps communicate scale and system logic in background architecture

SOURCE_NOTES:
- Observed from OSHA accident-prevention sign requirements and Brady safety sign guidance
- Sources used: OSHA 1926.200 and Brady sign standards overview, especially the visible hierarchy of danger/warning/caution bands and panel readability
- Exact wording and compliance detail are not the target; this card uses the visual system to support believable background design

CONFIDENCE: high
