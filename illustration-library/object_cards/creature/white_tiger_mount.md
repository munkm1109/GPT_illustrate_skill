NAME: white tiger mount
ALIASES: white tiger; pale tiger mount; 백호; 흰 호랑이; white tiger rider mount
CATEGORY: creature / tiger mount

FUNCTION:
- serves as the main support creature carrying the rider and establishing mythic authority, danger, and mobility
- must read as a powerful living animal first and a fantasy mount second

CORE_SILHOUETTE:
- massive feline body with low shoulder line, heavy forequarters, long striped tail, broad head, and clear scapula-to-hip rhythm
- saddle reads as a compact high-backed seat on top of a deep muscular barrel, not a flat horse-like platform

PRIMARY_FORMS:
- broad skull and cheek flare
- thick neck wedge into shoulder mass
- high shoulder block
- deep rib barrel
- narrower waist and strong hips
- long tail
- broad forepaws larger than rear paws

DIMENSIONAL_ENVELOPE:
- adult tiger body should feel longer than the rider's full seated body by a wide margin
- shoulder height should feel near or above the rider's seated hip height
- mount reads as low-slung and long, not tall like a horse

THICKNESS_NOTES:
- neck and shoulders are thicker than the hindquarters
- rib barrel is deep enough that a saddle pad must curve over a rounded body
- legs stay columnar and feline, never horse-thin

PROPORTION_RULES:
- forequarters dominate the power read
- spine line is gently sloped, not straight-backed
- stripes should wrap with volume, not lie flat like stickers

HUMAN_SCALE_READ:
- next to a teenage rider, the tiger should feel enormous and fully capable of carrying the weight without strain
- seated side-saddle rider should occupy only part of the back mass, leaving the tiger's shoulder and hip volumes readable

SUPPORT_LOGIC:
- rider weight sits over the ribcage just behind the shoulder block, never on the loin
- side-saddle pose needs the pelvis centered above the saddle tree while one leg drapes across the near side
- tiger stance should be calm and grounded, with all four paws planted or one forepaw slightly advanced

MATERIALS:
- dense fur with directional lay
- bright white coat with cool gray shadowing
- dark charcoal stripes
- leathery nose, glassy eyes, heavy paw pads

VISUAL_CUES:
- white coat plus wrapped stripes
- broad feline face and cheek ruff
- powerful shoulders
- thick tail

ANGLE_NOTES:
- side view is best for rider-on-mount relationship
- 3/4 side view keeps the face visible while preserving tiger body length

DO_NOT_BREAK:
- do not turn the tiger body into a horse back
- do not place the saddle too far back on the waist
- do not make the legs too long or thin

STYLE_VARIATION:
- realistic: visible scapula rhythm, fur direction changes, paw mass, stripe wrapping
- anime simplified: cleaner fur groups and reduced micro-detail, but preserve big-cat anatomy and weight
- reference-derived wrapper: eyes and face may sharpen, coat can take cool reflective edges, but the tiger must still feel biological and heavy

SCENE_USAGE:
- provides a diagonal carrying platform for the rider's silhouette
- supports noble, mythic, and dangerous mood without eroticizing the scene
- white fur contrasts strongly against ink-wash forest and dark saddle accents
