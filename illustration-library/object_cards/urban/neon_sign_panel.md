CATEGORY: urban / signage

FUNCTION:
- establishes urban nightlife identity and provides controlled color accents in dark street scenes
- acts as a depth marker and atmospheric light source more than as a primary narrative object

CORE_SILHOUETTE:
- rectangular or vertical sign panel with a luminous face and supporting bracket or frame
- reads as a glowing geometry suspended in the street canyon

PRIMARY_FORMS:
- sign face
- border frame
- bracket or mount
- luminous text/graphic field

DIMENSIONAL_ENVELOPE:
- can range from head-sized panels to multi-story vertical signboards, but in alley scenes should remain secondary to the human figure
- depth usually shallow compared to height and width

THICKNESS_NOTES:
- panel body should feel thin but rigid
- border frame slightly thicker than the glowing face
- brackets and mounts stay lighter than structural beams

PROPORTION_RULES:
- luminous face should dominate over frame thickness
- vertical signs should feel tall and narrow; box signs should stay compact and readable
- sign scale must fit the building rhythm and not float at arbitrary size

HUMAN_SCALE_READ:
- smaller nearby panels can feel arm-span to door-width scale; distant tower signs can exceed human height by several times
- should support the city corridor rather than overwhelm the main subject

SUPPORT_LOGIC:
- mounted to walls, poles, or bracket arms
- perspective and attachment must agree with the building plane
- glow should spill onto nearby wet surfaces and edges consistently

MATERIALS:
- metal frame
- acrylic or glass sign face
- internal neon/LED lighting
- bracket hardware

VISUAL_CUES:
- colored glow halo
- shallow panel body
- bracket attachment
- stacked sign rhythm through the street depth

ANGLE_NOTES:
- front: luminous text block and color read matter most
- side: panel thickness and bracket support become important
- 3/4: ideal for alley scenes because face glow and mounting logic both read

DO_NOT_BREAK:
- do not let signs float without support
- do not make every sign equally bright or equally saturated
- keep the main figure brighter in focal logic than any single background sign

STYLE_VARIATION:
- realistic: controlled glow bloom, weathered frame, subtle electrical dirt
- anime simplified: clear luminous block with minimal bracket detail
- cyber-gothic: stronger magenta/cyan contrast and dripping wet reflections
- redjuice-inspired: concentrated accent glow, broken reflective haze, and dark structural framing

SCENE_USAGE:
- ideal for depth rhythm in rainy urban scenes
- supports puddle reflections, edge lighting, and atmospheric perspective
- works best when distributed as a background light family rather than a single dominant object
