NAME: city crowd below
ALIASES: distant street crowd; people far below; tiny pedestrians; 시민들; 아래 군중; 거리 군중
CATEGORY: urban / crowd / scale marker

FUNCTION:
- provides human-scale reference at street level beneath rooftops, bridges, windows, or elevated structures
- communicates height, spectacle, danger, and public attention without requiring individual portrait detail
- supports the narrative that bystanders are watching the main action from far below

CORE_SILHOUETTE:
- clustered tiny heads, shoulders, silhouettes, umbrellas, phone glints, or upward-facing dots grouped along streets and sidewalks
- reads as a crowd mass with a few directional cues, not full-size background characters

PRIMARY_FORMS:
- small head dots / shoulder marks
- clustered body silhouettes
- sidewalk or street grouping
- phone screens, camera flashes, umbrella tops, or small face highlights
- directional gaze arrows implied by head tilt and body orientation

DIMENSIONAL_ENVELOPE:
- from rooftop height, each person compresses to tiny marks or small torso dots
- crowd clusters occupy sidewalk/street zones and should be much smaller than rooftop figures

THICKNESS_NOTES:
- individual body thickness usually collapses at distance; use grouped mass and tiny highlights instead
- umbrellas or signs can read as small disks/rectangles if weather or spectacle matters

PROPORTION_RULES:
- crowd scale must agree with the vertical distance and street plane
- do not render distant people with full facial detail unless the camera is near them
- density should gather along sidewalks, crosswalks, plaza edges, or street barriers, not random sky/background areas

HUMAN_SCALE_READ:
- useful as a scale ruler: if the main characters are on a high roof, the crowd should appear tiny and far below
- crowd size can exaggerate the height gap and make rooftop action feel dangerous

SUPPORT_LOGIC:
- crowd stands on street/plaza/sidewalk planes
- gaze direction can point upward toward the rooftop action
- barriers, crosswalks, cars, and street edges can organize clusters when needed

MATERIALS:
- fabric clothing masses, skin dots, phone glass glints, umbrellas, street reflections

VISUAL_CUES:
- tiny upward faces
- phone screen dots
- clustered silhouettes
- street/plaza placement
- small pointing arms or heads tilted upward

ANGLE_NOTES:
- high angle: crowd becomes dots/ovals on a street plane
- oblique view: use overlapping small silhouettes and sidewalk perspective
- far distance: use crowd mass texture with a few intentional highlights, but do not fake important objects

DO_NOT_BREAK:
- do not make distant citizens as large as rooftop characters
- do not place crowd marks on vertical building walls unless they are inside windows or balconies
- do not let crowd detail steal focus from main figures
- do not render random noisy dots without street-plane grouping

STYLE_VARIATION:
- realistic: more varied silhouettes, street barriers, and phone glints
- anime simplified: small dark clusters with a few upward faces and light dots
- gothic urban: mostly black crowd mass with pale faces/eyes looking up
- neon city: phone screens and signage reflections create small accent constellations
- redjuice-inspired: tiny controlled glyph-like crowd lights subordinate to the face/action

SCENE_USAGE:
- use for rooftop drops, disaster scenes, public duels, city chases, parade/stage views, and “look how high this is” compositions
- pair with `highrise_rooftop_edge`, `building_facade`, `window_grid`, and signage cards
