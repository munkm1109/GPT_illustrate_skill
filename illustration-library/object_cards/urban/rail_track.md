NAME: rail track
ALIASES: tram rail; train track; urban rail line; 철로; 레일
CATEGORY: urban / rail infrastructure

FUNCTION:
- defines the movement axis of the tram and the vanishing-line force of the scene
- anchors speed, direction, and city-corridor perspective

CORE_SILHOUETTE:
- twin parallel rails with repeating sleepers/ties and a strong perspective convergence into the distance
- reads as a narrow linear channel running through the city scene

PRIMARY_FORMS:
- left rail
- right rail
- repeating ties/sleepers
- ballast or roadbed / embedded street channel depending on setting

DIMENSIONAL_ENVELOPE:
- human scale is narrow enough that a person cannot stand comfortably between running rails without feeling compressed
- perspective stretch can dominate the depth axis of the whole image

THICKNESS_NOTES:
- rails should feel like steel strips with a small but real vertical profile
- ties repeat with thinner depth than the rails' longitudinal read

PROPORTION_RULES:
- rails stay parallel in reality and only converge through perspective
- tie spacing repeats rhythmically and tightens with distance
- tram width should clearly exceed the gap between the rails

HUMAN_SCALE_READ:
- rails should feel large enough for a tram but much smaller than the dragon body mass
- the corridor they create helps the city read as a chase/combat lane rather than an open plaza

SUPPORT_LOGIC:
- tram wheels run directly along the rail pair
- perspective lines of the track should support the vehicle travel direction and not drift independently of the tram body
- surrounding street or elevated structure should reinforce the path

MATERIALS:
- steel rails
- timber/concrete/embedded support logic depending on the scene

VISUAL_CUES:
- twin rails
- repeated ties
- vanishing perspective
- sparks, reflections, or speed blur aligned with the rail axis if dramatized

ANGLE_NOTES:
- front: strongest as a convergence cue beneath the tram
- side: best for showing longitudinal travel
- 3/4: most useful in action scenes because both depth and vehicle support stay readable

DO_NOT_BREAK:
- do not let the rail spacing change arbitrarily
- do not angle the tram contrary to the rail direction unless the scene intentionally shows derailment
- keep enough repetition to sell rail identity

STYLE_VARIATION:
- realistic: clearer tie pattern and steel wear
- anime simplified: two rails plus selective repeated ties and highlights
- gothic urban fantasy: darker roadbed, wet reflections, harsher perspective contrast
- redjuice-inspired: use luminous or high-contrast depth lines to intensify velocity without losing structural clarity

SCENE_USAGE:
- excellent for forcing the eye through the image depth and amplifying speed
- helps connect foreground dragon framing to the distant city corridor
- gives the action a believable transit-world base

SOURCE_NOTES:
