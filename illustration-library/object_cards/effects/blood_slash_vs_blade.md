NAME: blood slash separate from blade
ALIASES: blood arc; slash trail; motion slash; blade trail; red slash effect; 피의 궤적; 검격 효과; 베기 궤적
CATEGORY: effects / action mark / weapon interaction

FUNCTION:
- clarifies the difference between a rigid weapon blade and a fluid / motion / blood effect following the cut
- prevents swords, katanas, or other blades from bending into the red slash trail during image generation
- adds impact, direction, and violence while preserving weapon geometry and hand-arm alignment

CORE_SILHOUETTE:
- one rigid straight or intentionally curved weapon object plus a separate softer arc, spray, ribbon, or streak that trails behind or across the cut path
- the blade remains readable even when the effect is dramatic

PRIMARY_FORMS:
- rigid blade or cutting edge
- hilt / guard / hand contact
- effect arc or ribbon
- droplets, spray, or torn fragments
- impact point or wound edge when relevant
- negative gap or highlight separation between blade and effect

DIMENSIONAL_ENVELOPE:
- the blade length is constrained by weapon card and body scale
- the slash effect can extend beyond the blade but should follow the action arc and not replace the blade silhouette
- droplets and spray remain secondary marks except at the impact point

THICKNESS_NOTES:
- blade thickness stays thin, metallic, and structurally rigid
- blood/motion trail can taper, feather, break, or smear
- effect opacity can vary, but it should not swallow the hilt, hand, or blade edge

PROPORTION_RULES:
- blade remains narrower and more consistent than the effect ribbon
- the effect may be wider near impact and thinner at the tail
- the motion arc follows the swing path; it should not create a second impossible blade unless intentionally stylized and labeled
- if the weapon is straight, the visible blade edge should remain straight even when surrounded by curved effects

HUMAN_SCALE_READ:
- the effect scale should follow arm reach, sword length, and target distance
- a slash effect larger than the body can be acceptable in stylized action, but the underlying weapon and hand grip must still read at human scale

SUPPORT_LOGIC:
- effect originates from blade path, impact point, wound, or recoil direction
- hand, wrist, forearm, elbow, and shoulder should align with the weapon before effects are layered
- droplets should follow force direction or gravity, not random all-direction texture

MATERIALS:
- metallic blade, liquid blood, glowing motion smear, wet droplets, mist, sparks, or stylized paint-like streaks

VISUAL_CUES:
- clear blade edge
- separate red arc
- tapering trail
- impact cluster
- droplets of different sizes
- small negative gap or highlight between blade and effect

ANGLE_NOTES:
- front: blade/effect separation through value contrast is critical
- side: blade length and trail curvature are easiest to read
- 3/4: foreshortening can hide length, so preserve hilt-to-tip axis and separate the effect as a different plane
- extreme action: simplify the effect before sacrificing blade geometry

DO_NOT_BREAK:
- do not bend a straight sword into the blood arc
- do not let the red slash become the only visible blade
- do not hide the hands or hilt behind unstructured effect smear
- do not create multiple accidental duplicate blades from motion streaks
- do not scatter blood equally everywhere; cluster it at impact and action direction

STYLE_VARIATION:
- realistic: heavier droplets, darker blood mass, limited motion stylization
- anime simplified: clean red arc, a few droplets, strong silhouette gap
- gothic: darker wine-black blood and sharp torn streaks
- neon sci-fi: luminous edge trail or energy slash separated from physical blade
- redjuice-inspired: surgical crimson accent, rough splatter at focal trigger, hard white blade flare kept distinct

SCENE_USAGE:
- use whenever a sword, katana, claw, or blade is combined with red slash, blood spray, or motion trail
- pairs with `hero_sword`, `katana_sheathed`, `human_hand_fingers`, and action anatomy cards
- helpful in image-generation prompts to state: “blade is rigid and separate from the red slash effect”

SOURCE_NOTES:
- Workflow card derived from repeated image-generation failure mode where blade geometry collapses into action effects
- General action drawing logic: structure first, effect second, texture last

CONFIDENCE: high
