NAME: hero sword
ALIASES: one-handed sword; battle sword; heroine blade; 검; 소드
CATEGORY: weapon / blade

FUNCTION:
- serves as the decisive action prop that defines attack direction and cut force
- implies courage, precision, and heroic confrontation against a larger monster

CORE_SILHOUETTE:
- straight or gently tapered one-handed sword with a clear hilt/guard and a long cutting line extending beyond the hand
- reads as a slender bright directional axis rather than a heavy greatsword slab

PRIMARY_FORMS:
- blade
- guard
- grip
- pommel
- optional blood arc or motion streak after impact

DIMENSIONAL_ENVELOPE:
- about arm-length to slightly longer in draw-read logic
- blade length clearly exceeds hilt length by several times and stays much narrower than the forearm

THICKNESS_NOTES:
- blade must remain thin at the edge and rigid through the spine
- guard and pommel can feel slightly chunkier, but the overall object stays agile rather than massive

PROPORTION_RULES:
- hilt is short enough for one-hand use
- blade dominates the silhouette length
- guard should not widen so much that it distracts from the slash line

HUMAN_SCALE_READ:
- lighter and faster than a giant weapon; should feel suited to a nimble heroine moving atop a tram roof
- should extend the body gesture instead of overpowering it

SUPPORT_LOGIC:
- the sword should align with the swing arc or follow-through of the cut
- wrist, elbow, and shoulder should all support the blade direction
- if blood or impact is shown, it should reinforce the cut path instead of obscuring the blade silhouette

MATERIALS:
- polished steel blade
- leather or wrapped grip
- metal guard and pommel

VISUAL_CUES:
- long clean edge line
- reflective metal plane
- simple readable guard
- sharp attack direction

ANGLE_NOTES:
- front: keep highlights or edge separation so the blade does not disappear
- side: strongest for length and strike direction
- 3/4: best for action scenes because it preserves both foreshortening and silhouette clarity

DO_NOT_BREAK:
- do not make the blade too thick or club-like
- do not shorten it into a dagger if the scene is about a neck-severing strike
- keep the weapon readable against the background and creature body

STYLE_VARIATION:
- realistic: crisp steel reflections and tighter guard detail
- anime simplified: one clean blade mass with a decisive highlight band
- gothic: darker steel, blood-red reflections, sharper guard accents
- redjuice-inspired: use selective white flare and shard-like highlight planes without losing the sword silhouette

SCENE_USAGE:
- ideal for driving the eye through an action diagonal
- metal highlight can puncture dark dragon mass and urban shadows
- pairs well with cloak flow to form a heroic X or slash composition

SOURCE_NOTES:
- Observed from the finished reference: the heroine wields a one-handed sword while cutting through the dragon's neck area
- Construction notes are generalized from common single-hand battle sword proportions for drawability

CONFIDENCE: medium
