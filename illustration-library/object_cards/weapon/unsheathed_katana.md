NAME: unsheathed katana
ALIASES: drawn katana; black katana; Japanese sword; katana blade; 일본도; 뽑은 카타나; 검은 카타나
CATEGORY: weapon / blade

FUNCTION:
- serves as a rigid lethal action line that extends the arm gesture and defines pursuit, threat, or cut direction
- can carry cultural/fantasy identity through tsuba, wrapped handle, blade curvature, and lacquer/dark metal treatment

CORE_SILHOUETTE:
- long slender blade with subtle curvature, short handle, small guard, and a decisive hilt-to-tip axis
- reads as a blade, not a club, ribbon, or random black streak

PRIMARY_FORMS:
- blade
- cutting edge / spine
- kissaki tip
- tsuba guard
- tsuka handle wrap
- pommel/fitting
- optional scabbard separate from drawn blade

DIMENSIONAL_ENVELOPE:
- blade length clearly exceeds forearm and handle length
- overall sword often reads from hand to well beyond arm reach but remains human-wieldable
- thickness stays very thin relative to hand and forearm

THICKNESS_NOTES:
- blade is thin, rigid, and edge-like even when colored black
- guard and handle are thicker than blade but much smaller than a western crossguard silhouette
- wrapped handle creates repeated grip texture without becoming bulky

PROPORTION_RULES:
- handle is a small fraction of total sword length
- curvature is subtle; the blade should not bend like a whip unless stylized as a separate energy effect
- tip stays sharp and aligned with blade curvature
- black blade still needs a rim highlight or edge separation

HUMAN_SCALE_READ:
- suited to one or two hands depending on pose; should not become a giant fantasy slab unless explicitly requested
- hand grip, wrist, elbow, and shoulder must support the blade direction

SUPPORT_LOGIC:
- blade direction follows wrist, forearm, elbow, and shoulder action
- if held while running, it trails or points along a clear motion line
- if combined with glow or slash effects, the physical blade remains separate and rigid

MATERIALS:
- polished steel, blackened metal, lacquer-dark finish, wrapped grip, metal guard

VISUAL_CUES:
- subtle curved edge
- sharp tip
- small round/oval guard
- wrapped handle
- thin rim highlight on dark blade

ANGLE_NOTES:
- front: edge highlight is critical or the dark blade disappears
- side: curvature and length read best
- 3/4: preserve hilt-to-tip axis and avoid merging blade with sleeve, cloak, or effects

DO_NOT_BREAK:
- do not bend the katana into a motion trail
- do not make the blade thick like a club
- do not use a western crossguard silhouette
- do not hide the hilt/guard so completely that the sword becomes an anonymous black line
- do not let black color erase blade edge readability

STYLE_VARIATION:
- realistic: crisp metal edge, subtle hamon or edge transition, restrained reflections
- anime simplified: clean blade shape with one strong rim highlight
- gothic: black blade, cold white edge, dark metal fittings
- neon city: cyan/blue edge glint against black metal
- redjuice-inspired: surgical black wedge with thin white/cyan cut-light and restrained handle detail

SCENE_USAGE:
- use for hunters, samurai-inspired outfits, urban fantasy chases, rooftop duels, and fairy-tale inversions
- pair with `human_hand_fingers`, relevant body anatomy cards, and `blood_slash_vs_blade` when effects are present
