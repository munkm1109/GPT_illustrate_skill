CATEGORY: environment / ground plane / park

FUNCTION:
- support plane for seated or reclining figures in a peaceful outdoor scene
- provides soft texture, scale, and contact shadows without becoming dense architecture

CORE_SILHOUETTE:
- broad low horizontal plane with short blade clusters and uneven soft edge texture
- in perspective, grass texture compresses with distance and should not become vertical noise

PRIMARY_FORMS:
- ground plane slab
- grass blade clusters
- soft contact shadow under body
- optional small flowers/leaves as low-density accents

DIMENSIONAL_ENVELOPE:
- extends under the figure and beyond frame; grass height is small relative to hands/feet
- foreground blades can be taller; background grass becomes texture field

THICKNESS_NOTES:
- grass is a shallow surface texture; do not treat it as thick fur unless explicitly stylized

PROPORTION_RULES:
- blade clusters taper upward and vary in length; avoid equal comb-like strokes
- density should thin around focal face/hands to avoid competition

HUMAN_SCALE_READ:
- seated adult compresses grass under hips/legs; contact shadow and flattened patches prove support

SUPPORT_LOGIC:
- pelvis/legs rest on the ground plane; hands and sandwich can sit above lap; hair/coat may spill onto grass

MATERIALS:
- matte plant blades, soft earth shadow, occasional dew or dust

VISUAL_CUES:
- blade clusters, uneven color patches, small leaves, contact flattening, local shadow

ANGLE_NOTES:
- low camera: foreground blades larger and darker; background compresses into mottled field
- top/3/4 camera: show flattened oval contact patch around seated body

DO_NOT_BREAK:
- do not make grass a random green noise blanket
- do not let grass hide hand/sandwich contact
- do not let grass perspective conflict with body support

STYLE_VARIATION:
- anime simplified: broad green value field plus a few blade clusters
- grunge poster: desaturated green-gray mottling, scratch texture, black ink blade accents
- peaceful Huke-derived: use muted grass with rough paper texture so mood is happy but still graphic/grungy
