NAME: lab monitor
ALIASES: industrial monitor; panel mount display; rugged touchscreen; control display; 실험실 모니터; 산업용 모니터
CATEGORY: laboratory / industrial display

FUNCTION:
- displays measurements, camera feeds, warnings, access data, or experiment state
- implies system control, surveillance, process visibility, and machine logic

CORE_SILHOUETTE:
- flat rectangular screen with a sealed bezel
- either panel-mounted into a wall/machine face or attached to a stand/arm
- cable routing exits to the rear or through a protected mount cavity

PRIMARY_FORMS:
- display panel
- bezel or protective front frame
- mounting body or rear housing
- arm, bracket, wall plate, or panel opening
- cable exit or connector zone

DIMENSIONAL_ENVELOPE:
- compact panel monitor logic: roughly 220 x 150 x 45 mm
- common mid-size industrial monitor logic: roughly 380 x 307 x 48 mm
- large widescreen panel logic: roughly 520-560 mm wide x 320-360 mm high x 45-65 mm deep

THICKNESS_NOTES:
- bezel face usually reads about 15-30 mm around the screen
- rear housing depth commonly reads 45-65 mm, thicker than consumer displays
- mount arm or plate should feel thick enough to support 6-18 kg class hardware

PROPORTION_RULES:
- the visible screen sits inside a rugged outer frame, not edge-to-edge like a phone or tablet
- industrial monitors are shallow relative to width, but not ultra-thin
- rear housing and mount logic should feel proportional to a sealed worksite device

HUMAN_SCALE_READ:
- small units read like a clipboard-sized control face
- mid-size units read like a torso-width work display
- large units can sit beside a standing figure without exceeding shoulder width in most portrait scenes

SUPPORT_LOGIC:
- the screen must attach to a wall, arm, post, rack, or machine face
- panel-mount versions need a flush cutout or recessed frame
- stand-alone versions need a VESA arm, yoke, post, or enclosure mount
- cables should exit at the rear and route into a protected or believable connection path

MATERIALS:
- tempered glass or protective acrylic window
- coated metal or magnesium/aluminum bezel
- rear steel enclosure
- cable glands, sealed connectors, or mounting hardware

VISUAL_CUES:
- flat front face
- thick rugged bezel
- warning UI blocks or status bars
- protected rear cabling
- touch target overlays or industrial interface layout
- mounting bolts, side brackets, or VESA plate

ANGLE_NOTES:
- front: screen hierarchy and UI readability matter most
- side: bezel thickness, rear housing depth, and mount logic become critical
- 3/4: best for showing the monitor as part of the room rather than a floating rectangle

DO_NOT_BREAK:
- the monitor needs a believable mount
- the screen should not feel as thin as a phone if it is industrial/ruggedized
- rear connector logic should not be exposed randomly
- bezel and housing should feel durable enough for an industrial or lab environment

STYLE_VARIATION:
- realistic: thicker rear enclosure, sealed bezel, visible cable glands, minimal but functional UI
- anime simplified: keep the flat face and mount logic while reducing connector clutter
- gothic: use the monitor sparingly, perhaps with a framed shrine-like housing or emblematic UI shapes
- neon sci-fi: stronger glow bands, translucent overlays, sharper warning color blocks, cleaner cable management

SCENE_USAGE:
- useful as a medium-density information object near tanks, doors, or workstations
- can support worldbuilding with UI, numbers, or hazard states
- should sit below the face in contrast unless the monitor is intentionally the secondary focal point
- good for adding reflected cool light on nearby props
