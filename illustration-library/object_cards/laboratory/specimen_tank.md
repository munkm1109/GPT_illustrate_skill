NAME: specimen tank
ALIASES: containment tank; bio tank; stasis tank; specimen chamber; 표본 탱크; 배양 탱크
CATEGORY: laboratory / containment device

FUNCTION:
- holds a specimen, fluid, or experimental subject
- implies secrecy, danger, medical control, or suspended life
- can borrow real cryogenic vessel logic when a sci-fi chamber needs believable engineering

CORE_SILHOUETTE:
- tall vertical chamber
- readable as a cylinder or rectangular capsule
- thick top cap and heavy base
- top valve cluster, gauge band, or service head
- secondary hoses, clamps, or manifolds attached to the outer frame

PRIMARY_FORMS:
- main transparent chamber
- top cap
- bottom base
- support frame or collar
- valve / gauge cluster
- hose / cable attachments
- side control box or indicator strip

DIMENSIONAL_ENVELOPE:
- real portable cryogenic tank logic: roughly 250-600 mm diameter x 600-1500 mm height
- larger lab floor unit logic: roughly 500-900 mm width x 1400-2200 mm height x 500-900 mm depth
- human-display containment variant for illustration: often 0.35-0.55 human width and 0.9-1.3 human height for the outer frame if used as a secondary background object

THICKNESS_NOTES:
- transparent wall read: about 20-60 mm outer wall thickness in stylized cutaway terms
- top cap / base support should read thicker than the wall, often 40-120 mm visual thickness
- support frame ribs and collars should feel heavier than cable or hose attachments

PROPORTION_RULES:
- strongly vertical object; height usually reads 2-4x the visible chamber width
- top service zone stays compact relative to the main chamber
- base should read at least as stable as the chamber is tall; avoid tiny feet under a heavy tank

HUMAN_SCALE_READ:
- small dewar version reaches roughly knee to waist height
- medium wheeled tank reaches hip to chest height
- stylized specimen display chamber can reach shoulder to above-head height when used as a framing object behind a character

SUPPORT_LOGIC:
- the chamber must feel heavy and sealed
- the base is wider or heavier than the chamber wall
- pressure, fill, or vent hardware should gather near the top service zone
- hoses should connect to believable ports, not float in space
- mounted versions need a wall bracket or floor stand that visibly carries the load
- mobile versions should justify wheels, handles, or a rolling base

MATERIALS:
- glass or acrylic chamber
- steel or coated metal frame
- rubber or polymer tubing
- internal fluid
- gaskets or seals

VISUAL_CUES:
- condensation
- bubbles
- warning labels
- fluid line or fill level
- internal glow
- locks, clamps, or seal rings
- gauge face or valve handle near the cap

ANGLE_NOTES:
- front: symmetry, specimen visibility, and inner fluid level read best
- side: chamber thickness and port depth become more important
- 3/4: best angle for showing volume, hose routing, and layered transparency

DO_NOT_BREAK:
- chamber wall thickness must feel strong enough to contain pressure
- top and base cannot feel lighter than the central chamber
- hose entry points need mechanical logic
- the support base must visually justify the object standing upright
- service hardware must not feel randomly scattered

STYLE_VARIATION:
- realistic: thicker seals, more hardware, readable access ports, fluid residue
- anime simplified: reduce bolts and labels but preserve the large chamber + cap + base read
- gothic: more ornate clamps, cathedral-window proportions, iron-like ribs
- neon sci-fi: cyan or magenta fluid, LED strips, stronger glow and warning graphics

SCENE_USAGE:
- strong vertical framing object near the edge of the scene
- useful for adding scale and lab-world storytelling behind the character
- can bounce colored light onto skin or costume
- works well as a secondary focal anchor if kept darker than the face

- Sources used: Cryofab CL/CLPB liquid nitrogen tanks; Cryofab dewar container overview; Cryofab CF dewar flask notes
