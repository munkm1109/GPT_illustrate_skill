NAME: Joseon-style saddle
ALIASES: Joseon saddle; Korean traditional saddle; riding saddle; 조선식 안장; 말안장; 말갖춤 안장
CATEGORY: transport / saddle

FUNCTION:
- provides a believable riding seat and establishes Korean historical tack identity
- must read as a compact rigid saddle adapted for ceremonial fantasy riding rather than a modern equestrian seat

CORE_SILHOUETTE:
- short rigid saddle tree with raised front and rear guards over a padded seat, plus cloth and tack elements hanging at the sides
- reads as a perched seat mounted on a rounded animal back, not a soft blanket alone

PRIMARY_FORMS:
- front rise
- rear rise
- seat plank or padded top
- under-pad
- side flaps / hanging tack

DIMENSIONAL_ENVELOPE:
- length should fit only the rider pelvis and upper thigh support zone
- width is narrow compared to the mount barrel, leaving animal body visible on both sides

THICKNESS_NOTES:
- main tree is rigid and wood-based in historical logic
- pads and underlayers cushion the back but remain thinner than a modern padded saddle

PROPORTION_RULES:
- front and rear rises must be visible enough to keep the rider seated in side-saddle posing
- seat remains shorter than a couch-like cushion
- side hanging elements should not hide the entire flank of the mount

HUMAN_SCALE_READ:
- clearly sized for one rider
- should look capable of anchoring a teenage rider securely on a large tiger back without swallowing the rider visually

SUPPORT_LOGIC:
- saddle tree sits over the mount's strongest ribcage zone just behind the shoulder mass
- use an under-pad between saddle and animal body
- side-saddle rider must feel centered on the seat even if both legs drape to one side

MATERIALS:
- wood frame
- leather or lacquered leather exterior
- textile pad or saddle cloth
- metal fittings and hanging strap hardware

VISUAL_CUES:
- high front and back seat guards
- compact seat
- under-pad and side hanging cloth / leather elements

ANGLE_NOTES:
- side and 3/4 views best show the raised front/back silhouette
- top views flatten the tree and should be avoided for storytelling shots

DO_NOT_BREAK:
- do not use a modern western saddle horn silhouette
- do not place the seat too far back on the mount
- do not let the saddle read as a loose blanket

STYLE_VARIATION:
- realistic: wood-tree logic, leather covering, textile under-pad, strap details
- anime simplified: preserve high front/back silhouette, pad, and hanging side sheets with reduced hardware detail
- reference-derived wrapper: the saddle should provide a dark structural mass that supports the rider silhouette without overcompeting with the face

SCENE_USAGE:
- crucial for making the side-saddle tiger pose believable
- helps separate rider costume planes from animal anatomy

SOURCE_NOTES:
- Credible references: encyclopedia entries describe saddles as wood-and-leather riding equipment and horse tack as including saddle pad, under-pad, and side hanging protection elements such as 다래 / 장니
- Inference: this card adapts that historical Korean horse-tack logic to a fantasy tiger mount while preserving seat placement and structural readability
