NAME: cigarette
ALIASES: lit cigarette; smoking cigarette; filtered cigarette; 담배; 종이담배
CATEGORY: personal / smoking item

FUNCTION:
- reads as a small handheld prop that implies pause, defiance, fatigue, seduction, or controlled self-destruction
- in-scene it sharpens finger gesture and character attitude more than it adds mass

CORE_SILHOUETTE:
- a very thin straight cylinder, usually white or pale paper with a slightly warmer filter end
- can gain a tiny ember point and faint smoke tail when lit

PRIMARY_FORMS:
- paper-wrapped tobacco cylinder
- filter segment at one end
- ember or ash tip at the lit end
- optional smoke ribbon

DIMENSIONAL_ENVELOPE:
- draw-read logic: about finger-width thin and approximately the length of two adult finger joints laid end to end
- the object should remain much slimmer than the fingers holding it

THICKNESS_NOTES:
- keep the cylinder extremely slim and uniform
- the filter should read only slightly thicker at most, mostly by color or band change rather than size change
- ash should stay delicate and brittle, not club-shaped

PROPORTION_RULES:
- long-to-thick ratio must stay very high
- filter is a short end segment, not half the total length
- the object should sit lightly between fingers, never reading like a marker or stylus

HUMAN_SCALE_READ:
- should read thinner than the little finger and close to matchstick-to-chopstick-between scale
- even at stylized resolution it must feel fragile relative to the hand

SUPPORT_LOGIC:
- usually pinched between index and middle finger or held between fingertips
- because it is so light, gesture and finger spacing sell the object more than force or grip
- smoke, if added, should rise or drift with air rather than form a heavy ribbon

MATERIALS:
- white paper wrap
- tan or warm filter paper
- compacted tobacco / ash logic
- optional ember glow

VISUAL_CUES:
- tiny cylinder
- filter color break
- ash tip or ember dot
- faint smoke trace
- elegant finger pinch silhouette

ANGLE_NOTES:
- front: reads mainly by the tiny bright line and finger spacing
- side: easiest angle for full length and ember/filter separation
- 3/4: useful for embedding in hand gesture without flattening into a line artifact

DO_NOT_BREAK:
- do not make it too thick or too short
- do not overpower the hand silhouette with large smoke effects unless the scene specifically wants that
- the cigarette should support mood, not become the first focal object over the face

STYLE_VARIATION:
- realistic: subtle paper texture, ash irregularity, faint smoke diffusion
- anime simplified: clean white cylinder with a tiny ember and one smoke cue
- gothic: cooler ash, darker ember surroundings, more ritual calm in the hand pose
- neon sci-fi: retain the cigarette silhouette but let nearby colored light tint its paper edge

SCENE_USAGE:
- works as a psychological accent in close or medium portraits
- helps direct gaze from the face into the hand gesture and back again
- ideal when the character mood is detached, dangerous, exhausted, or quietly self-possessed
