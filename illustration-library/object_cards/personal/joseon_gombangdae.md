NAME: Joseon gombangdae
ALIASES: gombangdae; Korean tobacco pipe; Joseon tobacco pipe; 담뱃대; 곰방대; 연죽; 연관
CATEGORY: personal / smoking item

FUNCTION:
- works as a long elegant hand-and-mouth prop suggesting old-world status, composure, and slightly roguish refinement
- should extend the head angle and side profile without becoming the main focal point

CORE_SILHOUETTE:
- very long, thin, almost wand-like pipe with a tiny bowl at one end and a tapered mouthpiece at the other
- the total length strongly exceeds the size of the bowl

PRIMARY_FORMS:
- metal bowl / head
- long slender shaft
- mouthpiece tip

DIMENSIONAL_ENVELOPE:
- longer than a cigarette by a large margin and capable of spanning from hand to beyond cheek line in profile
- thin enough to feel precise and aristocratic rather than rustic and chunky

THICKNESS_NOTES:
- bowl remains small and heat-dense
- shaft stays extremely slim for most of its length
- end pieces may be slightly thicker and metallic

PROPORTION_RULES:
- bowl is tiny relative to total length
- shaft must remain the dominant silhouette element
- avoid western-pipe large-bowl proportions

HUMAN_SCALE_READ:
- long enough to feel ceremonial next to a youthful face
- should read lighter and thinner than most fingers

SUPPORT_LOGIC:
- can rest lightly between lips or be held with fingers near one end
- when at the lips, keep the angle shallow and elegant so it does not obscure the eyes
- smoke, if shown at all, stays minimal and subordinate

MATERIALS:
- metal bowl and mouthpiece
- bamboo or wood shaft

VISUAL_CUES:
- tiny metal bowl
- elongated shaft
- delicate straight read

ANGLE_NOTES:
- side profile best communicates length
- 3/4 side view lets the pipe point back toward the face without flattening

DO_NOT_BREAK:
- do not enlarge the bowl
- do not thicken the shaft into a stick or staff
- do not make smoke obscure the youthful facial read

STYLE_VARIATION:
- realistic: bamboo grain or dark lacquer shaft, small metal gleams, tiny ash cue
- anime simplified: one decisive line with tiny bowl and subtle metallic accents
- reference-derived wrapper: the pipe should act as a narrow directional line supporting the profile and gaze

SCENE_USAGE:
- excellent for side-angled rider portraits because it extends the profile silhouette
- should remain secondary to eyes, hair, and hat brim

SOURCE_NOTES:
- Credible references: encyclopedia and museum notes describe Joseon-era tobacco pipes as composed of bowl, long shaft, and mouthpiece, often with bamboo or wood shaft and metal ends; long shafts could signal status
