NAME: neon_lab_room
SCENE_TYPE: indoor laboratory portrait / containment room
MOOD_TAGS:
- cold
- tense
- clinical
- secretive
- neon

CORE_OBJECT_SET:
- specimen tank
- warning panel
- cable bundle
- lab monitor
- one major structural frame or wall module

OPTIONAL_OBJECTS:
- steel tray
- ceiling rail
- fluid canister
- access door
- hanging lamp or LED strip

LAYOUT_PATTERNS:
- place one tall vertical object near the side edge for framing, usually a specimen tank or structural chamber
- run cable bundles along wall, ceiling, or tank edges to connect large forms
- attach lab monitors to wall modules, side arms, or tank-adjacent control zones
- place warning panels on surfaces that logically control access or hazard zones
- place steel trays in lower side work zones or near benches so they support procedure without competing with the face
- keep one clear visual lane around the character's head so the face remains readable

FOCAL_SUPPORT:
- specimen tank works as a secondary vertical anchor behind or beside the character
- warning panels reinforce world logic and can echo accent color without becoming first read
- cable bundles should guide the eye inward, not pull it out of frame
- lab monitors can provide medium-value UI planes and cool light bounce
- steel trays add low, reflective procedural detail near secondary work areas

DENSITY_RULES:
- highest density around wall hardware, tank attachments, and lower side zones
- medium density in mid-background around control surfaces
- lowest density immediately around the face and major silhouette edges

LIGHTING_IMPLICATIONS:
- tanks can create colored glow and reflected rim light
- warning panels can add small red or amber accent points
- cable bundles stay mostly in darker middle values unless used as a leading-line highlight
- lab monitors can introduce flat cool light and small UI highlight blocks
- steel trays can catch low-angle reflections and reinforce horizontal planes
- background lighting should stay subordinate to face contrast

STYLE_VARIATIONS:
- realistic: more control hardware, more attachment logic, more wear and layered labels
- anime simplified: fewer panels and cables, stronger silhouette separation, clearer glow hierarchy
- gothic neon: specimen tanks become chapel-like vertical forms, cables become black vine-like routing, warning panels can carry emblematic icon blocks
