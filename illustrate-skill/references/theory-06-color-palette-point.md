# THEORY_06_COLOR_PALETTE_POINT

Summary: This theory applies a dark controlled palette over the established value structure and uses only one or two strong accent colors to lock focus and atmosphere. It treats color as a disciplined extension of value, not as a new competing structure.

Summary: Step 6 must decide the base tone, the support colors, the 1-2 accent colors, where those accents are allowed to appear, and how to preserve the value hierarchy from Step 3.

## Purpose

3단계 값 구조 위에 어두운 전체 팔레트와 1~2개의 강한 포인트 컬러를 안정적으로 얹는다.
감정과 장면 의도에 맞춰, 많은 색을 쓰지 않고도 시선 초점, 분위기, 캐릭터성을 색으로 고정하는 것이 목표다.

이 단계에서 먼저 결정해야 하는 것:

- 전체 분위기를 지배하는 베이스 톤
- 포인트 컬러 1~2개
- 강한 색이 들어갈 위치와 피해야 할 영역

## Basic concepts

### Base tone and accent color

- base tone: 전체를 지배하는 어두운 저채도 색조 그룹
- accent color: 고채도 또는 고명도 포인트 색

기본 규칙:

- 베이스 톤은 어둡고 통일감 있게 유지
- 포인트 컬러는 1~2개만 강하게 사용

### Main / support / accent structure

- main color -> 전체 분위기를 이끄는 주색
- support color -> 메인을 보완하는 주변색
- accent color -> 시선을 끄는 포인트

이 스타일에서는 메인/서브는 어두운 쪽, 악센트는 제한된 강색으로 운용한다.

## Emotion-to-color translation

감정을 색으로 번역할 때는 hue뿐 아니라 saturation/value도 함께 본다.

예:

- tension / danger / fear -> cool dark base + warm accent
- gothic / tragedy / mystery -> purple / blue-black base + white light or red accent
- quiet tension -> muted teal / gray-blue base with restrained accents

규칙:

- sadness -> lower saturation, colder and duller tone
- anger -> stronger red accent and slightly higher perceived heat
- fear -> colder tones with extreme contrast

## Palette construction rules

### Restricted palette

기본 구조:

- 2-3 base colors
- 1-2 middle/support colors
- 1-2 accent colors

규칙:

- limit the image to 3-4 hue regions at most
- only 1-2 colors should feel truly strong

### Accent selection

Preferred accent pool for this style:

- red
- teal
- purple
- white light

Example combinations:

- red + teal -> dangerous neon laboratory
- purple + white light -> gothic / sacred mystery
- red + white light -> pain, warning, martyr-like tension

## Value-color alignment

색은 Step 3의 값을 깨면 안 된다.

규칙:

- the brightest value remains the brightest even after recoloring
- dark regions may take strong hue, but must keep dark value
- base tones stay mostly in middle-to-dark value families

## Per-part color distribution

### Skin / hair / clothing / background

Skin:

- stays relatively warmer and softer than the base environment
- separates from the cooler world even when the whole scene is cold
- must not become glassy, porcelain, waxy, or plastic-beauty smooth
- should receive nearby darkness, hair shadow, prop shadow, bounced red/cool light, and local value variation so it belongs to the scene
- default anti-AI skin direction: prefer muted warm gray-beige skin integrated into the scene light and surrounding shadow, like `C:\Users\munkm\.codex\generated_images\019dbade-3529-7af2-a495-b916f1be9d70\ig_01a1a195de2efd960169ea38ca06a88191acf757f4dcc29332.png`, unless the user explicitly asks for another complexion/material style

Hair:

- can stay closer to the base palette
- may carry accent hints if they support the face

Clothing:

- mostly base-tone variation
- accent use should remain weaker than the face or eyes

Background:

- almost entirely base-tone territory
- accent colors appear only in small purposeful locations

### Accent placement priority

Priority order:

1. eyes / iris / highlight zone
2. hair accents, ribbons, accessories, selected shards
3. small background objects such as equipment lights, signs, neon strips

규칙:

- if the background carries more accent than the face, focus weakens
- accent area should generally stay below about 10-20% of the canvas

## Style-ready palette examples

### Pattern A: neon laboratory

- base: dark teal, dark blue, gray-blue
- skin: slightly warm gray-beige
- accents: teal neon in background devices, red in eyes / labels / warning notes

### Pattern B: gothic portrait

- base: dark purple, ink-black, blue-gray
- skin: pale cool ivory / beige
- accents: purple highlights and white light

### Pattern C: blood / warning emphasis

- base: dark blue, ink-black, dark brown
- skin: lightly warm beige
- accents: red and white light

## Step 6 execution summary

1. Re-read Step 1 intent and Step 3 value structure.
2. Choose 2-3 base colors.
3. Choose 1-2 accent colors from the allowed pool.
4. Assign:
   - main
   - support
   - accent
   roles.
5. Apply color while preserving the value hierarchy.
6. Distribute color by part:
   - skin
   - hair
   - clothing
   - background
   Include a non-plastic skin-color lock: skin hue/value must be affected by the scene light and neighboring shadow rather than floating as a separately beautified face layer.
7. Place accent color with priority at the face and eyes.
8. Check the image at reduced size and confirm that color supports focus and atmosphere instead of scattering it.

## Gate

Do not continue to Step 7 unless all are true:

- hue families stay limited to about 3-4 regions or fewer
- the base tone remains dark and coherent
- only 1-2 accent colors are strongly active
- the Step 3 value structure remains intact after color
- skin, clothing, and background separate by hue/saturation/material feel
- skin remains alive and scene-integrated rather than porcelain, glass, wax, or plastic
- the face and eyes remain the strongest color focus

If any item fails, revise the palette or accent placement before texture.
