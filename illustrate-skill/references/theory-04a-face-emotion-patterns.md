# THEORY_04A_FACE_EMOTION_PATTERNS

Summary: This auxiliary Step 4 theory converts abstract emotion labels into reproducible combinations of eyebrows, eyelids, pupils, highlights, and mouth shape. It is built for restrained facial acting rather than exaggerated cartoon expression.

Summary: Use this theory to map emotions such as cold smile, quiet tension, threatening calm, suspicion, and calm sadness into part-level facial settings that can be repeated consistently.

## Purpose

얼굴 감정을 추상어가 아니라 파츠 조합으로 변환한다.
특히 과장 대신 미세한 변화로 감정을 전달하는 스타일에서, 눈·눈썹·입의 조합을 안정적으로 재현하기 위한 보강 이론이다.

이 단계에서 해야 하는 일:

- 감정을 눈 / 눈썹 / 입의 상태 조합으로 풀어내기
- 큰 변형이 아니라 작은 각도와 거리 변화로 감정 강도를 제어하기
- 필요하면 아주 작은 비대칭으로 냉소, 의심, 위협감을 강화하기

## Core principles

### Expression lives in the situation

상업 일러스트에서 자연스러운 얼굴은 감정을 정답처럼 설명하지 않는다.
인물이 그 상황 안에 실제로 존재하고, 행동/소품/빛/시선에 의해 눌린 표정을 짓는 쪽을 우선한다.

금지 기본값:

- villain -> exaggerated grin
- strong -> wide power stare
- sexy -> inflated lips or staged seduction mouth
- authority -> obvious looking-down scowl

대신:

- restrained observation
- lived-in boredom / fatigue / calm
- small eye-lid pressure
- tiny mouth tension
- prop-held or shadow-interrupted expression

### Emotion reads as combination, not one part

같은 입꼬리라도 눈이 부드러우면 친근한 웃음이 되고, 눈이 가늘고 동공이 작으면 조소나 위협이 된다.
같은 반쯤 감긴 눈도 눈썹 상태에 따라 무표정, 권태, 경계로 달라진다.

규칙:

- 감정은 눈 / 눈썹 / 입 세 그룹으로 나눠 조합한다

### Micro-adjustment over exaggeration

이 스타일에서는 작은 변화가 핵심이다.

예:

- eyebrow angle shift
- eyelid openness shift
- mouth-corner offset

기본 규칙:

- 큰 찌푸림, 큰 입 벌림, 과도한 치아 노출은 기본적으로 금지

## Part-level emotional meaning

### Eyebrows

- near horizontal -> calm, cold, restrained
- inner brow lowered -> anger, focus, threat, vigilance
- inner brow raised -> sadness, anxiety, wavering
- outer brow raised -> suspicion, sarcasm, intellectual superiority

### Eyes / eyelids

- more open -> tension, alertness, surprise, anxiety
- half-lidded -> coldness, boredom, superiority, threatening calm
- sharp outer contour -> aggression, resolve, coldness
- lower-lid tension -> compressed emotion, restrained intensity

### Pupils / highlights

- smaller pupil -> sharpness, tension, aggression, concentration
- larger pupil -> surprise, vulnerability, openness
- larger / more highlights -> openness, warmth, emotional availability
- smaller / fewer highlights -> coldness, distance, lack of softness

### Mouth

- near straight line -> restraint, calm, control
- tiny upward shift -> confidence, sarcasm, controlled smile
- tiny downward shift -> fatigue, sadness, suppression
- slight parting -> breath, tension, hesitation, seduction depending on context

규칙:

- 입은 감정의 설명문이 아니라 해석 보정 장치다

## Emotion pattern presets

### Cold smile

- brows: near horizontal, slight outer lift allowed
- eyes: half-lidded
- outer eye contour: slightly sharp
- pupil: medium to small
- highlights: small and controlled
- mouth: short thin slight rise

### Quiet tension

- brows: slight inward pull or mild inner drop
- eyes: slightly more open than neutral
- lower lid: light tension
- pupil: slightly small
- highlights: small and clear
- mouth: almost straight, slightly firm

### Threatening calm

- brows: almost unchanged, near horizontal
- eyes: half-lidded
- pupil: small
- highlights: very limited
- mouth: straight or very faint upward shift

### Suspicion / distrust

- one outer brow slightly raised
- gaze slightly off-center or side-directed
- possible mild eyelid asymmetry
- pupil: medium
- mouth: asymmetrical micro-rise or near straight

### Calm sadness

- inner brow slightly raised
- eye emphasis slightly lower and heavier
- lower lid: fine tension
- pupil: medium to large
- highlights: softer
- mouth: faint downward curve

## Emotion mixing

현실적인 얼굴은 종종 단일 감정보다 혼합 감정으로 읽힌다.

기본 규칙:

- use `main emotion 70% + support emotion 30%`
- eyes usually carry the main emotion
- brows often carry the support or direction cue
- mouth stabilizes the final interpretation

예:

- danger + allure
- anxiety + restraint
- sadness + pride

## Asymmetry rule

완전 대칭은 차갑고 정적이며 AI식 정면 미소 일러스트처럼 보일 수 있다.
사람 얼굴은 항상 대칭적이지 않으므로, 액션이 과하거나 감정이 과하거나, 혹은 적은 감정이라도 상황을 표현하는 데 얼굴이 중요할 때는 작은 비대칭을 먼저 시도한다.

허용:

- one brow slightly higher
- one mouth corner slightly lifted
- one lid slightly more compressed
- head tilt or chin angle breaking perfect frontal symmetry
- hair shadow, pipe, hand, cigarette holder, mask edge, or prop crossing the face and interrupting perfect facial polish

금지:

- 좌우가 전혀 다른 표정처럼 보일 정도의 과한 비대칭
- eyes pointing in mismatched directions unless intentionally designed and supported by the scene
- asymmetry that looks like anatomical error rather than acting

이 스타일에서는 아주 작은 비대칭이 냉소, 의심, 위협에 특히 효과적이다.

## Step 4 execution summary

1. Extract main and support emotion from the scene intent.
2. Decide intensity: low / medium / high.
3. Pick brow state first.
4. Pick eyelid openness and gaze direction.
5. Adjust pupil size and highlight amount.
6. Place the mouth last to lock interpretation.
7. Add minor asymmetry when it strengthens the intended reading or prevents a too-perfect AI face.
8. Check the face at reduced size for restrained readability.

## Gate

Do not pass Step 4 unless all are true:

- emotion reads from eye / brow / mouth combination rather than mouth alone
- brow angle and lid state agree with the emotional direction
- pupil size and highlight amount match the intended intensity
- the mouth supports rather than over-explains the emotion
- any asymmetry reads as controlled design, not as an error
- the face avoids direct cliché acting for villainy, power, seduction, or authority unless explicitly requested
- the final read feels like a person inhabiting the situation rather than an expression sticker pasted onto the face

If any item fails, revise the emotional pattern before moving forward.
